Jay: Privacy policy

Version 1, effective 2026-11-14.

Jay is an end-to-end encrypted messenger. We built it so that we know as little about you as possible: you do not need a phone number or an e-mail address, and we cannot read your messages, calls, group names or files. This policy says exactly what the service does store, why, for how long, and who helps us run it.

3. What we never have

  • The content of your messages, calls, files, group names and backups. They are encrypted on your device with keys that never leave it (Signal protocol: X3DH and Double Ratchet).
  • Your address book. Contacts are added by QR code or invite link only.

4. Processors

  • Microsoft Azure (Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd.), region West Europe: the API server, encrypted file and backup storage, and the web app.
  • Hostinger: the virtual server running the PostgreSQL database.
  • Cloudflare: TURN relay for calls when a direct connection is not possible. Calls stay end-to-end encrypted (DTLS-SRTP); the relay sees IP addresses and data volume only.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) and Apple Push Notification service (iOS): delivery of content-free notifications.
  • The push service of your browser (for example Mozilla, Google, Apple or Microsoft) if you allow notifications in the web app.

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU (for example in the USA). Such transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with the Czech supervisory authority, the Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).

  • Access and portability: in the app, Settings > Privacy > Export my data downloads everything the server holds about you as JSON.
  • Erasure: Settings > Account > Delete account, or see Delete account for other ways.
  • For anything else write to info@zcloud.cz with your handle. We answer within one month.

6. Children

Jay is not directed at children under 15. If you are younger, do not use it without your parent or guardian's consent.

7. Security

Besides end-to-end encryption, transport is protected by TLS, passwords are hashed with Argon2id, and the local database on your device is encrypted. Known limits of the encryption are described honestly in the app. Security issues: security.txt.

8. Changes

We announce significant changes to this policy in the app at least 14 days in advance. The version and date are at the top of this page.
