Frequently asked questions

Do I need a phone number or e-mail?

No. Every device gets a random handle. An e-mail is only needed if you want cloud backup.

How do I add a contact?

Scan their QR code or open their invite link. Jay never reads your address book.

Can you read my messages?

No. Messages, calls, files and group names are end-to-end encrypted. The server only passes on encrypted envelopes and deletes them after delivery (at the latest after 30 days).

I lost my phone. Can I get my chats back?

Only if you registered and turned on backup, or have another linked device. Without that, nobody (including us) can recover them.

Someone is bothering me.

Open the chat menu and choose Block; they can no longer write or call you and are not told. Choose Report to send us a report; you can attach the messages in question.

I don't get notifications.

Allow notifications for Jay in the system settings and keep battery saving from stopping the app. Notifications say only "New message"; the content is shown when you open the app.

How do I create my own bot?

Settings > Bots > New bot. A detailed step-by-step guide for Claude, ChatGPT and the Hermes agent: Bot guide.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete account.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Settings > Privacy > Export my data.
