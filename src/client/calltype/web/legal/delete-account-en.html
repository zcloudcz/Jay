Jay: Delete your account

Version 1, effective 2026-11-14.

You can delete your Jay identity and account at any time. This page explains how, including without the app.

1. In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Jay and go to Settings > Account > Delete account.
  2. Read what will be deleted, then type your handle (CT-XXXX-XXXX) to confirm. A registered account also asks for its passphrase; with the app lock on, you confirm with your PIN or biometrics.
  3. The app deletes the data on the server, then everything on the device, and starts from the beginning.

2. In the web app

If you use Jay in the browser, open https://jay.zcloud.cz/#/settings/account/delete and follow the same steps.

3. By e-mail

If you no longer have the app or the device, write to info@zcloud.cz with your handle (CT-XXXX-XXXX) and, if you registered, from the account's e-mail address. Because Jay has no phone number or password reset by e-mail for anonymous identities, we may ask for proof that the identity is yours (for example a message from one of your contacts' chats). We delete the data within 30 days and confirm it.

4. What is deleted

  • Your device identity, handle, public keys and pre-keys, and on the main device also all linked devices.
  • Your account (e-mail, password hash, two-factor secret) and all encrypted backups.
  • Group memberships, mutes, blocks, push tokens, undelivered messages to and from you, and your uploaded files.
  • Channels you own.

5. What is kept

  • Messages your contacts already received stay on their devices; we cannot reach them.
  • Reports you filed stay as moderation records without your contact e-mail, and are deleted after 180 days (user reports).
  • Moderation decisions about channels are kept as a record required by Regulation (EU) 2022/2065.
