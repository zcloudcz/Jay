# Jay: Channel and bot rules

Version 1, effective 2026-11-06. Czech version (binding in case of doubt): `pravidla-kanalu.md`. Web: `https://jay.zcloud.cz/legal/channels-en.html`.

## 1. Scope

Jay is an end-to-end encrypted messenger. Private conversations (1:1 and groups) are encrypted so that the operator cannot see, read or moderate their content. These rules therefore apply only to **channels**: public one-way channels that are not end-to-end encrypted and that anyone can follow. They apply to channel owners, their admins and publishers (including bots).

Operator: Martin Zahálka, company ID (IČO) 07783809, Havlíčkova 1550/54, 680 01 Boskovice, Czech Republic ("operator").

## 2. What is not allowed in a channel

Each rule has an identifier (K1 to K10). We cite it whenever we act against content.

- **K1 Illegal content.** Content or activity that is illegal under Czech or European Union law, in particular child sexual abuse material, terrorism and violent extremism, offering illegal goods and services (drugs, weapons, forged documents), human trafficking.
- **K2 Violence and threats.** Credible threats, incitement to violence, coordinating violent or criminal activity, instructions for or encouragement of suicide or self-harm.
- **K3 Hate and harassment.** Attacking people or inciting hatred based on race, ethnic origin, nationality, religion, sex, sexual orientation, disability and similar characteristics; targeted harassment and bullying of specific people.
- **K4 Fraud and deception.** Phishing, scams, obtaining money or data by deceit, impersonating another person, company or authority.
- **K5 Spam and manipulation.** Bulk unsolicited content, chain messages, artificially inflating reach, repeatedly posting identical or empty content.
- **K6 Privacy.** Publishing another person's personal data without consent (address, phone number, documents), intimate images without consent.
- **K7 Intellectual property.** Content you have no rights to (others' works, trademarks), unless the law permits the use.
- **K8 Adult and graphic content.** Channels are public and must be suitable for everyone: no pornography, sexual services, gratuitous violence or graphic imagery.
- **K9 Honest channel.** A channel's name, description and content must match; a channel must not mislead about who is behind it.
- **K10 Bots and automation.** Automatically published content may only be added through a bot the owner added as a publisher; Jay labels it visibly. A bot must respect rate limits, and the channel owner is responsible for its posts as for their own.

## 3. Who is responsible

- The **owner** (the registered account that created the channel) is responsible for all channel content, including posts by admins, publishers and bots.
- An **admin** may edit the channel profile, publish and delete posts.
- A **publisher** (person or bot) may only publish and delete their own posts.
- The owner may delete the channel at any time.

## 4. How to report content

- **In the app:** choose "Report" on a channel or post, pick a reason (illegal content, rule violation, spam) and briefly describe the problem. For illegal content, explain why you believe it is illegal and confirm that you report in good faith. Optionally leave an e-mail address for the outcome.
- **Without the app:** e-mail `martin@zcloud.cz` with the channel handle (`CH-XXXX-XXXX`) or post, a description, and your name and e-mail.
- We never disclose the reporter's identity to the channel owner or admins.
- We confirm receipt immediately and tell you the outcome in the app (Reports I filed) or by e-mail.

## 5. How we decide

- Every report is reviewed by a person (the operator). We use no automated removal or automated decisions.
- We decide diligently, objectively and proportionately, with due regard to freedom of expression.
- Possible measures, mildest first: (1) no action, (2) removing one post, (3) suspending the channel for a fixed time (hidden, no posting), (4) terminating the channel.
- Where we suspect a criminal offence that threatens a person's life or safety, we inform law enforcement.

## 6. Statement of reasons and redress

- For every measure the channel owner receives a statement of reasons in the app: what we did and for how long, why (rule K1 to K10 or the law), whether it followed a report, and how to object.
- If you disagree, e-mail `martin@zcloud.cz` within 6 months citing the decision. We will review it again and tell you the result. This does not affect your right to go to court or to contact the Czech Telecommunication Office as Digital Services Coordinator.
